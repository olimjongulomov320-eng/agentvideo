# Recommendations — from demo to finished film

## 1. What the demo proves (keep)

* The visual system works: the firm's **own navy/gold/cream palette** with a serif
  display face reads premium, calm and credible at 1080p.
* The **9-scene, 2:23 structure** holds the requested arc (beginning → history →
  achievements → people → new office → forward) without feeling rushed.
* The **honesty-on-screen** approach (labels for unconfirmed statements, an
  illustrative-mockup tag, a persistent demo marker) is what makes the film safe to
  show; keep it in the final cut wherever a claim is not independently verified.
* The **closing cream card with the real logo** is the strongest single frame — a
  clean, confident finish.

## 2. What to fix first, in priority order

1. **Decide the factual open questions** (`PLACEHOLDERS.md` §C) and, above all,
   resolve the **website's unverified claims**. This is the top reputational risk.
2. **Get archive + new-office + event photography.** Photographs are the single
   biggest quality upgrade — they convert three typographic beats (2010, 2024,
   new office) into real history.
3. **Record the narration professionally** — Uzbek master first, then Russian.
   The demo's Russian TTS is only a pacing guide.
4. **Replace the music** with a licensed cue (or commission) once the picture is locked.
5. **Human visual QC of the photo crops.** The portraits are framed by an automatic
   rule; a person must confirm no face is awkwardly cropped.

## 3. Craft refinements worth doing (cheap, high impact)

* **Scenes 1 and 7**: refine the abstract motifs, or replace them with real imagery
  once supplied.
* **Scene 5 (Wakil AI)**: with a real screen capture and an approved query, this
  becomes the film's signature moment. Consider a short screen-recording of the answer
  typing out and the citation appearing — restrained, no gimmicks.
* **Scene 6 (people)**: if team photos vary in background, shoot a short consistent
  portrait session (same light, same background) — it will look far more intentional than mixed
  sources.
* **Typography**: if the firm adopts brand fonts, re-render the titles to match the
  website exactly.
* **Subtitles**: decide burn-in vs. sidecar per platform (sidecar SRTs are provided
  for both languages).

## 4. Formats to also produce for the finished film (already scripted)

* A **45 s 9:16 social cut** and a **15 s teaser** — written in `production/07`.
* **Burned-in Uzbek and Russian** subtitle masters for platforms that need them.
* **Interview inserts** of the founder and Prof. Gulyamov if the client wants voices
  other than the narrator (`production/03`, `production/04`).

## 5. Process recommendations

* **Lock the facts before locking the picture**, so no re-edit is needed after
  verification.
* **Get written permissions** for all portraits and the Tahota name on file.
* **Keep a single source of truth** for timing — the demo uses `build/timeline.py`;
  continue to drive both picture and subtitles from one file so they never drift.
* **Re-render, don't re-invent**: the whole film is reproducible with five commands
  (`demo/README.md`); swap assets, re-run, and the demo updates to match the final
  material.

| 2. Supply archive/new-office/event photography + real Wakil AI capture | client | asset pack (drop in `archive-input/`) |

# Narration — GSP LAW opening film DEMO

Two parallel texts, both timed to the same picture. The **Uzbek** is the intended
master read (to be recorded by a human narrator). The **Russian** is the version
used by the temporary synthesized voice in `GSP_demo_RU.mp4`.

Facts are restricted to what the research pass could support; anything sourced only
to the firm is spoken/handled as such (and labelled on screen). No client matter,
figure, award or credential from the firm's website is used.

## Delivery notes for the final voice-over

* **Language:** Uzbek first (master), Russian second. Record Uzbek first so the
  Russian can match its pacing; keep both at the same emotional arc.
* **Pace:** unhurried and authoritative; the demo recorded the Russian at ≈165 wpm
  and the picture was cut to that. Sentence-level pauses are already implicit in the
  scene cuts — do not rush the 2010 and Wakil AI lines.
* **Pronunciation (Russian):** the temporary voice used respellings that a human
  narrator will not need — *«Вакил Эй-Ай»*, *«Стэнфорд Кодэкс»*, *«лекс точка уз»*,
  *«Тахота»*. For the final read, agree preferred pronunciations with the client.
* **Words to treat carefully (accuracy):** always "с 2010 года" / "2010-yildan" —
  never a spoken "16 years"; "одной из первых" / "birinchilardan bo'lib" — never
  "the first"; state Tahota and the summit as the firm's own reports if the client
  has not supplied independent documentation.
* **Voice:** one narrator throughout; the founder and Prof. Gulyamov may appear as
  optional spoken inserts (see `production/04_shot_list.md`), but the film must stand
  alone without them.

## B. Missing materials to request from the client

1. **Archive photographs (2010–2012)** — office, founders, early documents, any
   press. Highest-value gap: they would replace the typographic 2010 beat with real
   history and make the "journey" section authentic.
2. **New-office photographs/video** of Mirobod ko'chasi 46a — exterior, interior,
   the moving-in. (Request as early as possible; a shooting window is simplest.)
3. **Event photographs** from the 2024 Tahota partnership and the Al Baraka
   Islamic-finance summit — needed if scene 4 is to show the event rather than a motif.
4. **A real Wakil AI screen capture** (or screen recording) — plus a decision on which
   query/answer may be shown publicly (client-privilege check).
5. **Logo source files** — ideally SVG/AI and an approved light-on-dark version (the
   current PNG is dark-on-transparent only; if a dark-background logo lockup exists it
   would help).
6. **Exact opening date/format** for 20 October 2026 (public opening? press? guests?)
   so the closing and any call-to-action are correct.
7. **Credited team list** — confirm which people may be shown and their exact titles
   (the site and the film currently say "Sherik/Advokat", etc.).
8. **Permission confirmations in writing** for the founder's portrait, all team
   portraits, and the Tahota name — currently granted verbally in-session.
9. **The screenshot placed in `archive-input/`** — its content could not be read by
   this model; if it carries anything the film should reflect, please describe it.
10. **Brand typography** — the site uses a plain system sans; if the firm has brand
    fonts (or wants a specific serif), supply them so the film and the site match.
    The demo currently uses Georgia (display) + Helvetica Neue (labels).

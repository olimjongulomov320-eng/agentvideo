# Concepts — GSP Law Firm

## concept-a — The Distance (recommended)

_Justice is delayed by distance and time; the firm closes that gap, and now builds legal AI to close it faster._

- Evidence fit: Strong (uses only what the ledger supports; gaps become placeholders)
- Strengths: Emotionally legible in every language; Works without archive photos; Sets up an AI/technology climax honestly
- Weaknesses: Depends on one milestone being verifiable; Needs an evocative opening image

## concept-b — Built by Hand

_A firm built case by case — the story is people, habits and standards, not slogans._

- Evidence fit: Medium (strongest if interviews are recorded)
- Strengths: Human and warm; Ideal if clients/team clips exist
- Weaknesses: Weak without interview audio; Risk of drifting into praise

## concept-c — The Record

_An evidence-led film: dates, documents and milestones shown as a timeline._

- Evidence fit: Strong and safest (closest to the ledger)
- Strengths: Verifiable on screen; Credible for a legal audience
- Weaknesses: Coolest tone; Fewer emotional peaks

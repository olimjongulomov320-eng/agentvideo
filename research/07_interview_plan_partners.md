He can uniquely testify to the **partnership side from 2019** and the **IT/cyber-law direction**. He is an academic, so ask for specifics, not theory.

1. When and how did you join the firm — what year, and what convinced you? What did you want to build together?
2. You are a professor and a practitioner. What can a law firm do that a university cannot — and vice versa?
3. What in Uzbekistan's legal system changed in the last decade that made this firm's specialisation necessary?

5. What is the risk to a law firm that embraces AI — and how does this firm manage it?
6. What should never be automated in legal work?
7. What does the move to a new office mean to you personally?

*Follow-ups:* "What year?" · "Who was involved?" · "Is there a document or photo from that period?"

## Tier 1 — the origin (highest value)
1. In 2010 the firm begins. Take me back to that year — where were you working, who were your first partners, and what made you start a firm of your own?
   - *Follow-ups:* Who else was there? What was the first office like? What was your first real case?
2. What was the hardest moment in those early years — the one that nearly stopped you?
3. In 2019 Prof. Gulyamov joins the partnership side. What actually happened that year — a formal partnership, a new direction, a new name?
   - *Follow-up:* How did you and Prof. Gulyamov meet? Whose idea was the partnership?
4. The firm has carried the name "Sadikov" since the start. What does that name mean to you personally?

## Tier 2 — identity and turning points
5. Your firm's channels say "15", "16+", "20+ years" at different times. What is the true number, and what does it measure — your practice, or the firm?
6. Can you recall a specific moment when you knew the firm had crossed into a new stage? What happened, who was involved?
7. Which case — one we can discuss publicly or in anonymised form — shows what this firm is really for?
8. Who shaped the firm's culture most, and through what *specific action*, not speeches?
9. What principle have you never abandoned, even when it cost you something? Tell me about one time it cost you.

## Tier 3 — the AI chapter and the new office
10. You spoke at Tashkent Law Spring 2025 on AI in a lawyer's work. What did you see that most of your colleagues didn't?
11. You then co-founded **Wakil AI** (listed by Stanford CodeX, presented at the Academy of Justice). What made you *build* it, not just talk about it?
    - *Follow-up:* Whose idea? What was the first version? What surprised you?
12. What did the old office mean to you — and what does moving to the new one mean?
13. Why *now*, in 2026, is this the right moment to move?
14. What do you want the firm to be in five years — and what should it never become?

## Reserved follow-ups
- "What date was that?" · "Who else was there?" · "Is that documented?" · "Who could corroborate it?"
- "Can we say that publicly?" · "Does that involve client confidentiality?"

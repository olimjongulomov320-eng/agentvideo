## Standing rules for this project
1. A document being available in an archive never equals approval for public release.
2. Private messages are not copied into public-facing deliverables.
3. Sensitive research material is kept separate from approved production material.
4. Where a case could make a powerful story, first determine whether it can be told **anonymized / generalized / authorized**.
5. Unresolved legal/permission issues are marked explicitly. No definitive compliance claims without grounds.
6. Final public cut is subject to the firm's approval before release.

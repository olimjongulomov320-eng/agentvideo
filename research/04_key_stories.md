## Story A — "The hard choice, 2010"
- **Working title:** The founders who chose the narrow path
- **Time / place:** 2010–2012, Tashkent
- **People involved:** Abdulloh Sodiq (founder; formerly Ruslan Sadikov — ex-prosecutor); the firm name also joins Prof. Said Gulyamov
- **What happened (factual):** A small team establishes "Gulyamov, Sadikov and Partners" and deliberately limits itself to **economic disputes and B2B corporate work**, instead of general legal services.
- **Why it matters (meaning):** Specialisation, at a time when it was uncommon, is the firm's founding discipline.
- **Evidence:** `[PB-16, PB-17, PB-01]`
- **Confidence:** CORROBORATED (firm's own, multiple channels)
- **Confidentiality status:** publicly tellable
- **Emotional core:** conviction; doing the harder thing
- **Can it carry 20–35s of screen time?** Yes
- **Visual possibilities:** 2010-era photographs, early office, first documents

## Story C — "The advocate who built an AI" (VERIFIED centrepiece)
- **Working title:** Wakil AI
- **Time / place:** 2026, Tashkent (built on `lex.uz`)
- **People involved:** Abdulloh Sodiq (+ development partners)
- **What happened (factual):** The firm's leader co-founds **Wakil AI**, an Uzbek legal-AI assistant that answers in Uzbek and cites `lex.uz`. It is listed in the **Stanford CodeX TechIndex** and presented at the **Academy of Justice**.
- **Why it matters:** A practising advocate, not a tech company, helped build the tool — externally validated by Stanford and the government.
- **Evidence:** `[PB-07, PB-11, PB-04]`
- **Confidence:** **VERIFIED**
- **Confidentiality status:** publicly tellable, subject to **consent**
- **Emotional core:** restlessness; service at scale
- **Can it carry 25–30s?** Yes — it is the film's centrepiece
- **Visual possibilities:** the live product, the founder demonstrating, cleared CodeX / Academy screen inserts

## Screening test before a story becomes a video candidate
- [x] Specific person, place, moment
- [x] Corroborated or clearly qualified
- [x] Publishable (or anonymizable)
- [x] Reveals something about the firm's character
- [x] Does not require fabricated detail to work

## Explicitly NOT used
- Any client matter, criminal case, or "won case" detail — including publicly reported cases and the firm's own unnamed "victory" posts. **Confidentiality/privilege outweighs storytelling.**

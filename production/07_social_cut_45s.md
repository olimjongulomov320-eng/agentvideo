## Production notes
- **Hook discipline:** the first 1.5s must already show motion and text. No logo intro.
- **Text:** large, high-contrast, upper-third safe (avoid the bottom 20% where platform UI sits).
- **Sound:** works muted via burned-in subtitles; add a 2–3s music sting that matches the opening film's theme.
- **Pace:** cut on the beat; each scene is one idea.
- **Facts:** identical to the master — no new claims, no year count spoken, founder named **Abdulloh Sodiq**.
- **Do not** show client documents or the founder's former name.

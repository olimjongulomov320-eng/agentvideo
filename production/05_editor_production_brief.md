# 05 — Editor / Production Brief

**Version:** v1.0 (2026-10-15)
**Master:** **2:26** · **Languages:** **Uzbek (primary master)** + Russian version · **Screening:** new-office opening, 20 Oct 2026

---

## Visual language
Restrained and premium. **Real documents, real people, real software, real rooms** — no stock footage, no generic "law" imagery (no gavels-as-decoration, no glowing globe, no handshake). Two visual worlds edited against each other: **paper** (files, corridors, stamps) and **screen** (code, `lex.uz`, queries). They converge in Scene 5 and physically unite in Scene 7.

## Camera & composition
- Interviews (optional): 50mm, eye level, soft natural key; identical across subjects.
- Archive: slow moves and dissolves only — never a whip or zoom on a photograph.
- New office: movement that crosses thresholds; the camera walks in as a person would.
- Negative space is welcome. Silence is welcome.

## Music & pacing
- Single restrained theme (piano / muted strings), 70–80 bpm.
- Music **enters in Scene 2**, opens in **Scene 5** (the turn), resolves in **Scene 8**.
- Duck VO over interview audio by ~6 dB; never let music fight a quotation.
- No percussive "corporate epic" build.

## Transitions
- Cuts on meaning, not on beat.
- Dissolve only for time passage (Scene 2 founding; Scene 7 old→new).
- One **hard cut** at Scene 1 (paper → screen) — the film's signature move.

## Typography & on-screen text
- One clean serif for dates and titles; generous letter-spacing.
- Dates appear as small, quiet stamps, not big kinetic type: **2010 · 2011 · 2012**.
- Firm name set once, large, at the close.

## Colour
- Paper world: warm, slightly desaturated, filmic.
- Screen world: cool, clean — but not blue-teal cliché.
- Grade so the two sit in one frame without jarring.

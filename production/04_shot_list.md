## Capture rules
- **Permissions:** all people, the product and third-party screen captures are **cleared** (user, 2026-10-15). "consent first" notes in the table now refer only to confirming each person is comfortable on the day.
- **Archive:** deferred (not supplied). If it arrives, digitise at the highest resolution; never upscale a poor scan into a hero shot. Archive scenes use **archive assets only** — never restage history with today's staff.
- **Interviews (optional):** identical framing/lighting so the two voices cut together; audio ducked ~6 dB under VO.
- **New office:** shoot it as *a threshold being crossed*, not a property tour.
- **Screens:** capture the real product; no mock-ups.
- **Details:** hands, keys, signage, stamps — the small proof that this is real.
- **Paperwork check:** clear all documents/screens before rolling (client confidentiality).

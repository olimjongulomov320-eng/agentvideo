# Archive Input — drop folder

Place here any photos, scans, documents, PDFs, or text exports you want me to examine.

## How to use
1. Copy files into this folder (subfolders welcome, e.g. `photos-early/`, `documents/`).
2. Tell me in chat roughly what they are (approximate year, who/what is depicted) — or let me ask.
3. I will read them (images and PDFs directly; video **cannot** be viewed — see below) and log each one in `../research/09_visual_archive_catalog.md`.

## Important limits
- **Images:** readable.
- **PDFs of text:** readable.
- **Audio/video files:** I **cannot** play or transcribe them. For those, provide either a transcript, a written summary, or key frame images.
- **Screenshots of private chats:** please avoid unless necessary; private material is kept out of public deliverables.

## Permission reminder
Tell me for each item whether it is cleared for public use, needs consent, or is restricted. Nothing with unclear rights goes into the public film.
